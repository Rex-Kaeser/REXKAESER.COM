## Peer review (Touchpoint 5)

A peer reviewer read the page and left detailed feedback, logging my response to each point here, including the one I'm not taking.

**Background color and mood, not changing it.** I decided to run with the reviewers idea that the dark palette reads as foreboding, even though it was originally because the light colors hurt my eyes. I decided against a river-blue or brown palette. I decided the readability boost of the page not being overly bright was more important.

**Capitalization on section headers, valid catch, queued for a fix.** "The denial culture" should be title case to match the rest of the page ("The Denial Culture"), and checked for any other capitalization failures.

**More images of wildlife and the Yurok people.** The reviewer pointed to yuroktribe.org as somewhere I can get more images. I looked into this before the first draft and deliberately didn't pull photos of Yurok people or ceremonies from the Tribe's own site, because I couldn't find if I was allowed to source them, which I know I could with USGS/NOAA/NASA photos I did use, which are public domain and straightforward to verify. I did still add more photos but just not from the pointed source.

**Everything else, no changes.** The notes on tone, the video, and the interactive widget were positive; nothing to revise there.
